Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 30, 2026

We believe privacy pages should be understandable by actual humans, not just lawyers. This policy explains what we collect on TubZi, why we collect it, and what choices you have.

What We Collect

We collect limited information to run the site and improve performance. Depending on your usage, this can include:

  • basic technical details such as browser type, device type, pages visited, and approximate region;
  • platform activity, such as recently played games, favorites, and session-level interactions;
  • account-related information if you choose to sign in (for example, profile metadata from an auth provider).

We do not ask for sensitive personal data like government IDs, payment card numbers, or private messages outside site functionality.

How We Use Information

We use collected data to keep TubZi stable, secure, and useful. Typical uses include:

  • loading pages and games correctly on different devices;
  • diagnosing bugs and preventing abuse/spam;
  • understanding which features are helpful so we can improve the product.

We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and Local Storage

Like most web platforms, TubZi uses cookies and local browser storage to remember preferences (for example theme, recently played, and some feature state). If you clear your browser data, some of these settings or local progress may be lost.

You can control cookies through browser settings and extensions, but some features may stop working as intended if all storage is blocked.

Third-Party Services

TubZi uses third-party providers for parts of the platform, such as hosting, analytics, advertising, authentication, and embedded game content. Those providers may process data under their own privacy policies and terms.

We only integrate services we believe are appropriate for running and improving TubZi, but we cannot control third-party policy updates.

Public and Shared Information

If a feature includes public usernames, room codes, or shared game interactions, information you choose to submit there may be visible to others by design. Please avoid posting private personal details in public fields.

Data Security

We use reasonable technical and operational safeguards to protect data. No online system can guarantee perfect security, but we continuously work to keep TubZi and related services safer over time.

Children's Privacy

TubZi is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect unnecessary personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data inappropriately, contact us and we will review and address it.

Your Choices

You can:

  • clear cookies/local storage in your browser;
  • disable certain tracking tools at the browser or device level;
  • contact us to request review of account-related data concerns.

Policy Updates

We may update this policy when features or legal requirements change. If we make material changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of TubZi after updates means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

If you have privacy questions, contact us at tubzi@mail.com or via our contact page.
