About TubZi

Free browser games, curated for quick sessions and school-friendly fun.

TubZi is a lightweight game portal: we collect popular titles you can play right in your browser with no installs and no hassle. Our goal is simple: make it easy to find something fun, stay updated through TikTok and Discord, and grow the library based on what the community asks for.

Community

Have a game you want on TubZi? Say hi on TikTok or Discord. We read suggestions and use them to plan new additions.
