Advertise with us

Partnerships, sponsorships, and brand campaigns on TubZi.

TubZi works with selected partners whose products and messaging fit our audience and platform values. We prioritize experiences that feel useful, relevant, and non-disruptive for players.

What We Offer

  • brand placements tied to high-traffic pages and game discovery flows;
  • sponsored features or custom campaign integrations when appropriate;
  • social support opportunities through our active community channels.

Good Fit

We are generally open to campaigns that are gaming-adjacent, family-friendly, and transparent about promotion. We may decline categories that conflict with platform standards or user trust.

How to Pitch

Email tubzi@mail.com with:

  • your company/brand name;
  • campaign goals and timeline;
  • target audience and budget range;
  • any required deliverables or creative constraints.

You can also use our contact page or message us on Discord. If there is a fit, we will follow up with next steps and available options.
