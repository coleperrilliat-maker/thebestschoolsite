DMCA / copyright

TubZi responds to valid notices of alleged copyright infringement as required by law.

Filing a notice

If you believe content on or linked through TubZi infringes your copyright, please send a notice that includes:

• Identification of the copyrighted work
• The URL or location of the material on TubZi
• Your contact information
• A statement of good faith belief that use is not authorized
• A statement under penalty of perjury that your notice is accurate
• Your physical or electronic signature

Contact

Send DMCA correspondence to tubzi@mail.com or through our contact page. We may remove or disable access to material when appropriate.
