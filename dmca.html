DMCA / copyright

TubZi respects creators and responds to valid copyright notices.

If you believe content available on TubZi infringes your copyright, send us a proper DMCA notice and we will review it promptly.

How to File a DMCA Notice

Your notice should include enough detail for us to identify both the copyrighted work and the material you want reviewed. Please include:

  • your full name and contact details (email plus any relevant business information);
  • identification of the copyrighted work you believe is being infringed;
  • the exact URL(s) or clear location of the allegedly infringing material on TubZi;
  • a statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorized;
  • a statement under penalty of perjury that your notice is accurate and you are authorized to act;
  • your physical or electronic signature.

What Happens After We Receive a Notice

After we receive a valid notice, we may remove or disable access to the identified content while we review the claim. We may also notify the affected user, publisher, or provider when appropriate.

Counter-Notices

If you believe content was removed by mistake or misidentification, you may submit a counter-notice. Counter-notices should include your contact details, the removed material, and a statement made under penalty of perjury supporting your claim.

Repeat Infringement Policy

Where appropriate, TubZi may limit or terminate access for users or partners who repeatedly violate intellectual property rights.

Contact

Send DMCA notices to tubzi@mail.com or through our contact page. Use "DMCA Notice" in the subject line so we can route your request faster.
