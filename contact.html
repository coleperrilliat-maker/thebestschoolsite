Contact

Need help, want to suggest a game, or interested in a partnership? Reach out here.

We read feedback regularly and use it to improve TubZi. The quickest way to contact us is email, but you can also find us in Discord and on TikTok for announcements and community discussion.

General Support

If something is broken (a game not loading, a link issue, visual bug, etc.), include:

  • the page URL;
  • the game name;
  • your device and browser;
  • a short description of what happened.

That helps us reproduce and fix issues much faster.

Partnerships and Business

For sponsorships, brand partnerships, or media inquiries, include your company name, campaign idea, and timeline in your message so we can route it correctly.
